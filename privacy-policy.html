DriveMusicPlayer Privacy Policy (English)

Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance (Limited Use)

DriveMusicPlayer's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

1. Data Accessed

  • Google Account Sign-In & Google Drive (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly): Used solely to authenticate your Google account and stream audio files and folder/file metadata from your Google Drive in read-only mode. DriveMusicPlayer never modifies, uploads, or deletes files in your Google Drive.
  • Local Audio Files (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO): Used to scan and play audio files stored locally on your device when you grant permission.
  • Local Cache & Library Database: Playback caches, artwork, library metadata, and app preferences are stored locally on your device using Android Room Database and SharedPreferences.

2. No Third-Party or Developer Server Transmission

  • DriveMusicPlayer communicates directly and solely with Google Drive APIs and your local device storage.
  • Your personal data, account credentials, library metadata, and audio files are never transmitted to, sold to, or shared with the developer or any third-party servers.
  • DriveMusicPlayer does not include advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or third-party data brokers.

3. User Control & Data Deletion

  • You can clear the local audio/artwork cache, reset the local library database, or sign out of your Google account at any time from Settings inside the app.
  • Signing out revokes app access to your Google account session and clears local account state.
  • You can also revoke DriveMusicPlayer's access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account Security settings (Third-party apps with account access).
